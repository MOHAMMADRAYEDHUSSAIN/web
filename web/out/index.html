What is an AI voice receptionist?
An AI voice receptionist is software that answers phone calls in a natural voice, responds to common questions, and books appointments or routes calls to staff.
What is missed-call text-back?
Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends a text message to anyone whose call goes unanswered, so the conversation continues by text.
What is RAG?
RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) is a way of building AI that looks up answers in your own documents before it responds, instead of guessing.

FAQ

Questions about AI receptionists and automation

How much does an AI receptionist or automation project cost?

Pricing depends on scope: how many workflows you need, which tools we connect, and your call volume. RHTECH AI gives you a written quote after a free 15-minute discovery call, before any work starts. Many businesses begin with one workflow, such as missed-call text-back, and add more once it works.

How long does it take to go live?

Timing depends on scope and on how quickly we can access your phone system, calendar, and CRM. RHTECH AI starts with one workflow so you see a working system early. Your written plan includes a timeline before you commit, so you know the launch date up front.

What technology does RHTECH AI use?

RHTECH AI builds with current large language models, voice AI platforms, and standard web frameworks, and picks tools based on your needs and budget. We connect to the phone system, calendar, and CRM you already use where possible, so your team keeps the software it already knows.

Is my customer and patient data secure?

We connect only the systems a workflow needs, and we explain where data is stored and who can access it before launch. RHTECH AI does not claim HIPAA certification. If you handle patient health information, we review your requirements on the first call and plan the setup around them.

Can the AI be customized for my business?

Yes. Every system is built around your services, hours, pricing rules, and tone of voice. You approve the greeting, scripts, and answers before launch. The AI follows the rules you set, such as which calls to book, which to transfer, and which questions it should never answer.

Will an AI receptionist give me a return on investment?

We cannot promise a specific return, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What we can do is measure what the system handles: calls answered, texts sent, leads followed up, and appointments booked. You see those numbers and judge the value for your business yourself.

Does it integrate with my CRM and calendar?

In most cases, yes. RHTECH AI connects AI receptionists and follow-up automation to the CRM, calendar, and phone system you already use. On the discovery call we check your current tools and tell you plainly if something cannot connect, before you pay for anything.

Will the AI receptionist sound robotic?

Today’s AI voices sound close to natural, and you choose the voice and approve the script before launch. The receptionist answers common questions, books appointments, and hands the call to a person when the caller asks or the question falls outside its rules. You can review call summaries anytime.
